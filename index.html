About AUTO TECH

We're a new software company in Iraq with one goal: give local businesses systems that actually get used, every day.

Many businesses still run on paper, notebooks and scattered spreadsheets. We replace that with clear, simple software built around the way your team already works.

Management systems are our core. AI comes in where it saves real time, not as decoration.

Practical first

We build what solves a daily problem, then improve from real use.

Built around your team

Screens and steps match how your staff work, so adoption is easy.

Grows with you

Start with what you need now and add modules as the business grows.
